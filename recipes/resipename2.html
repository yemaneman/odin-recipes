steps

  1. Prep the vegetables: Finely chop the onion and carrot, and wash the salad greens.
  2. Boil the pasta: Bring a pot of water to a boil, add a generous pinch of salt, and cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions until al dente.
  3. Cook the base: Sauté the chopped onion and carrot in a pan with a little oil until softened.
  4. Combine and serve: Drain the spaghetti, toss it with your cooked vegetable base, season with additional salt to taste, and serve fresh alongside the salad.
